Import from Codex

Looking for Codex sessions…

Every session the Codex CLI recorded becomes a conversation here, with its original date and workspace. Running this again only picks up what is new, so nothing is imported twice.

Imported chats are readable history: tool calls come across as text rather than as replayable tool cards, and Codex's internal reasoning is left out because it cannot be replayed in a new conversation.
